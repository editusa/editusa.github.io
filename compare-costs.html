Estimates use only the amounts entered. They do not include unentered taxes, future price changes or cancellation charges.

How the comparison works

Each total is the monthly price plus required monthly extras, multiplied by the number of months, then added to any one-time fees. Choose the period you expect to keep the plan. The tool compares entered cost; it does not judge which service offers better access or quality.

The prefilled figures are hypothetical. Plan A costs $12 a month with a $24 setup charge; Plan B costs $16 a month with no setup fee. Over six months they both total $96. Shorter and longer periods produce different results. These figures are not current commercial offers.

What to enter

Use the regular monthly amount for a fixed-price plan. Put a mandatory recurring add-on in the monthly extras field and a setup or activation fee in the one-time field. Include taxes in your entered amounts if you want them counted. Keep optional purchases outside the comparison unless you intend to buy them.

If a plan has a temporary introductory price, a minimum contract, an annual payment or a variable bill, this simple model may not represent it. Calculate the actual payment schedule separately. A monthly equivalent of an annual fee does not describe when the money leaves your account.

Keep access and terms beside the total

Confirm that both options can do the work you need. A streaming service that cannot show the event in your region or software that cannot export your file is not a usable substitute merely because it costs less. Read renewal and cancellation terms on the provider's current page.

Your entries stay in this browser tab. This tool does not send them to Edit USA, store them in browser storage or contact a pricing service. Printing uses your browser's normal print dialog.

Use the same period.

A monthly offer and a discounted annual payment are different commitments. Keep the total cost, payment timing and minimum term in view.
